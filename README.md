<h1> Project Title </h1>
<p> AD Venture </p>

<h1> Project Description </h1>
<p> AD Venture is a comprehensive platform designed to integrate businesses, users, and administrators into a unified advertisement ecosystem. The goal of this platform is to provide an efficient, personalized,and interactive system for managing and engaging with advertisements. </p>
<p> From this platform, users can:
</p>
Businesses (Advertisers):<br>
-Create and manage targeted ad campaigns based on demographics, preferences, and location.<br>
-Monitor ad performance using real-time analytics, such as views, clicks, and feedback.<br>
-Update business profiles and manage posted ads (edit, pause, or delete campaigns).<br>

<p>Front Users (General Audience):</p>
-View personalized ads tailored to their interests and location.<br>
-Save favorite ads to a "Wishlist" for later review.<br>
-Search and filter ads by category, location, price, or popularity.<br>
-Provide feedback on ads through ratings or comments.<br>
-Update profile information and manage preferences for ad categories.<br>

<p>Admins:<p>
-Review and approve ad postings to ensure platform compliance.<br>
-Oversee platform-wide analytics and generate trend reports.<br>
-Moderate flagged content and handle disputes between users and businesses.
-Configure platform settings like ad categories.
